My Son's Toy Xylophone

Eight bars: D E F G A B C D. Tap the bars, or press 1–8 or A S D F G H J K on a keyboard.

I recorded every bar of my son’s toy xylophone and mapped each note’s real pitch, so this plays what his toy actually plays. We use it at home — his mum, his nanny and I — to learn a song before we sit down with the real bars, and to keep him playing along with us.

Plug in a USB piano keyboard and use Chrome or Edge. The white keys D E F G A B C D play bars 1–8. Any D at or above D5 plays bar 8.

These pitches were measured from recordings I made of my son’s own 8-bar toy xylophone. "Our toy's tuning" plays it exactly as measured, slightly flat in places. "Perfect tuning" plays every note exactly on pitch.
