About

Engineer by training, product manager by accident. I work on live‑ops at Zynga, which mostly means deciding what to put in front of players, and when.

Before that I spent four years on the other side of the “offline” fence — product and analytics for brands running loyalty and retention programmes — and a couple of years before that as a business analyst, mostly writing SQL and being genuinely surprised by what the data said.

The through-line is a preference for the unglamorous middle: the framework that decides what ships, the model that says whether it worked, and the discipline of getting that answer in front of the people who can do something about it. I’d rather build the thing than present the thing.

About

I grew up in Tamil Nadu, went to engineering college for a mechanical engineering degree I don’t use, and ended up in Bangalore building live‑ops for a mobile racing game. Every one of those steps was a detour that turned out to be the point.

Outside work I referee football, which I’m qualified to do and about which I have strong and mostly unpopular opinions. I host quiz nights. I do the cooking at family weddings, which in my family is a tradition rather than a talent.

I’m a cyclist, an amateur cook, and a reliably bad chess player. I like long walks, long flights, and getting a bit lost in a city I don’t know yet.

Ask me about Tamil food, football, or why live‑ops is harder than it sounds.
